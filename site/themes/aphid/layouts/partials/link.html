{{- /* One href from the front matter, made ready for the baseURL of the site. relURL adds the path of the baseURL to a relative address, and leaves an address that starts with "/" as it is, so the first "/" comes off before the call. The site is at the root of its domain today, and this keeps the links correct if it moves under a path. An address of another site, and an anchor on this page, are already what they must be. The menu of the header does not come through here: Hugo adds the path to a menu URL itself. */ -}} {{- $url := . -}} {{- if or (strings.HasPrefix $url "http") (strings.HasPrefix $url "#") -}} {{- $url -}} {{- else -}} {{- relURL (strings.TrimPrefix "/" $url) -}} {{- end -}}
